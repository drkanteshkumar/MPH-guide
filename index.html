About me

I grew up in Tharparkar, Sindh, studied in Hyderabad, and graduated in medicine from Aga Khan University in Karachi. After working as a physician and research associate at AKU, I applied to MPH programs in the US.

I applied to Harvard, Johns Hopkins and Columbia and was accepted to all three. I received the Presidential Scholarship at Harvard, covering full tuition, and the Dean's MPH Scholarship at Johns Hopkins. I'm now doing my MPH in Quantitative Methods at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health.

My research focuses on trauma and emergency care systems, with an interest in climate and disaster preparedness research.

I made this site because the same questions kept coming up in my DMs. I hope it saves you time.
