# How to apply

**Watch my video on the MPH application process:** [Watch on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeiOJWqBukD/)

## 1. SOPHAS

SOPHAS is the central application system for US schools and programs of public health. It has four sections. The first three are filled out once and shared with every program you apply to. The fourth is specific to each program, and that's where you answer each school's own questions and upload its statement of purpose or essays.

## 2. Credential evaluation (for Pakistani applicants)

Your transcripts need to be evaluated by WES (World Education Services). In my experience, the steps were:

1. **PMDC:** get your PMDC certificate, provisional or permanent. HEC needed it before attesting my degree.
2. **HEC attestation:** get your degree and transcripts attested by HEC, which issues e-attested documents.
3. **WES:** order a course-by-course evaluation. WES sends it directly to SOPHAS.
4. **SOPHAS** shares it with every program you apply to, so you don't need to send it separately.

Requirements can change, so check WES's "Preview Your Required Documents" tool on the WES website before you start.

## 3. English tests and GRE

- If your medium of instruction was English, WES can confirm it, and many schools accept that in place of IELTS or TOEFL.
- Many programs waive the GRE for physicians.

Check each school's policy, as these vary.
