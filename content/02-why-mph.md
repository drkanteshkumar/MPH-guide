# Why an MPH (and who it's not for)

An MPH opens a pathway into public health. But public health is broad, covering epidemiology, biostatistics, health policy, global health, environmental health and much more. The degree alone won't build a career for you. You'll need to put in real work and hours beyond your coursework to get something out of it.

**It is not an easier alternative to the USMLE.** It's a different path, and a difficult one on its own. An MPH in the US is expensive and takes time, money and effort to get into and to complete.

**An MPH makes the most sense if:**

- You're genuinely interested in public health, research or policy
- You want skills like data analysis, study design or program evaluation
- You have a clear idea of what you want to do with it, or are willing to figure it out actively

**Think twice if:**

- You mainly want a way out of something harder
- You're hoping it guarantees a job or a US career on its own
- You haven't thought about how you'll fund it
